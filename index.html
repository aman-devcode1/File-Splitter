    Frequently asked questions

    Does splitting change my file?

    No. Byte parts are exact ranges of the original bytes. Joining them in order gives back the identical file.

    Can I open a single part?

    Text files, yes. For images, video, audio, PDF and ZIP, a single byte part usually will not open. Structure and playable modes give parts that open on their own.

    Why are KB and KiB different?

    KB is 1,000 bytes and KiB is 1,024 bytes. Both are available in the unit list.
